## Evidence integrity

- Treat synthetic data and fuel/CO₂ figures as simulated or estimated, never as physical vehicle measurements.
- Keep generated datasets, credentials, `.env` files, and database volumes out of Git.
- A passing CI run proves only the jobs and assertions it actually ran. It does not prove load, cloud, availability, or full security targets.
- Save screenshots and benchmark outputs under `evidences/` with the tested commit, environment, and date.
