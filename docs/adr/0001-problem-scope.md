# ADR 0001 Establish the Fleet Intelligence Platform product scope

- Status: Accepted for the initial MVP
- Context: The workspace is the Fleet Management project, and the challenge is open-ended. The project should have a coherent fleet domain while the first slice stays achievable and measurable.
- Decision: Establish the product as the Fleet Intelligence Platform, turning connected-vehicle data into trustworthy, explainable fleet decisions across vehicles, trips, operational alerts, and costs. Use sustained idling with an estimated fuel impact as one early demonstration workflow.
- Consequences: The initial journey is concrete while the domain can grow into maintenance, utilisation, and fleet-wide reporting. Fuel savings are estimates until calibrated with validated fleet data; predictive maintenance and driver scoring are deferred.
